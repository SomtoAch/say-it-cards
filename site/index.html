Your deck

You can upload a CSV, TSV or text file (up to 2 MB), or drag one onto the box. Exports from Anki (Notes in Plain Text) and Quizlet work too.

Put one card on each line in the form question | answer. If more than one answer counts, separate them with /, for example car | coche / carro.

Privacy

Your decks and settings are saved only in this browser, on this device. This site has no accounts and never receives your cards, your answers or your voice. It counts visits anonymously with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn’t track you across sites.

Turning your voice into text is done by your browser’s own speech service. Chrome and Edge send the audio to Google or Microsoft to do this; Safari may do it on your device. If you’d rather not use the microphone, you can type your answers instead.
